Sales Policy & Protocols

Thank you for your interest in Good River LaManchas. Our priority is the health of our herd and placing our animals in quality homes.

Herd Health & Biosecurity

Our herd is strictly managed for optimal health and production. We maintain a regular testing schedule for CAE, CL, and Johne's. Copies of our most recent herd test results are available upon request to serious buyers.

Reservations & Deposits

We accept reservations on upcoming kiddings. There is no charge to be placed on our waiting list. Once a kid is born or an adult animal is offered for sale, a 50% non-refundable deposit is required to hold the animal. If the animal is not picked up within the agreed-upon timeframe, the deposit is forfeited.

Registration & Transfer

All animals sold as breeding stock will be registered with the American Dairy Goat Association (ADGA). We will provide the application for transfer or the original certificate upon full payment. The buyer is responsible for ADGA transfer fees.

Guarantees & Liabilities

We guarantee that any animal sold is healthy and free of visible disease or defects at the time it leaves our property. Because we cannot control the environment, management, or transit stress once the animal leaves our farm, all sales are final.

We reserve the right to retain any animal or cancel a sale at any point prior to pickup, in which case any deposit paid will be fully refunded.
