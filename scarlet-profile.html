Good River RP Scarlet

Click on picture for full pedigree.

Scarlet is a homebred doe! We love her for her balance, femininity, and dairyness. She’s long and level over her topline, with a smooth shoulder and sharp withers. She shows angularity and openness through the rib while maintaining depth and capacity. Overall, a clean, dairy doe that puts together a very complete and competitive package.
